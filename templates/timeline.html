JANUARY 1959 — PREPARATION
Expedition assembled at Ural Polytechnic Institute
Ten members selected. Route planned toward Otorten. Equipment checked. Dyatlov records an unexplained sense of unease in his personal diary prior to departure. [Historical core + fictional diary note]
25 JANUARY 1959
Departure by train from Sverdlovsk
Group travels by train to Ivdel, then onward toward the settlement of Vizhai. Spirits high. Photographs taken. Messages sent home confirming expected return within 2–3 weeks.
27 JANUARY 1959
Arrival at Vizhai settlement
Final supplies obtained. Local guides consulted. The group begins the trek into the mountains the following day.
28 JANUARY – 31 JANUARY
Trek begins — Checkpoint records
Progress logged at successive points. Weather remains manageable. One member, Yuri Yudin, experiences joint pain and decides to turn back. The remaining nine continue. Photographs of the farewell are taken. [Yudin historically returned; CTF expands checkpoint inconsistencies]
1 FEBRUARY 1959
Last known records — slope of Kholat Syakhl
Final diary entries and photographs recovered from the tent site. The group camps on the slope rather than in the forest. No further contact is made. Some later accounts mention reports of unusual lights in the northern sky that night — these remain unverified.
MID-FEBRUARY 1959
Failure to return — Families raise alarm
Expected return date passes. Families contact the Institute. Initial response is delayed. Search operations eventually authorized.
26 FEBRUARY 1959
Tent discovered by search party
Abandoned tent found on the slope. Cut open from the inside. Belongings and some footwear left behind. Tracks lead downhill toward the forest. [Core historical finding]
LATE FEBRUARY – MAY 1959
Bodies recovered — Investigation concludes
Bodies located in stages. Official cause of death recorded as hypothermia for most; additional injuries noted for others. Case closed. Certain files later described as incomplete or restricted. [Historical outcome; CTF adds restricted report discrepancy]
ARCHIVE RECOVERY — PRESENT
Digital reconstruction of case materials
Recovered documents, photographic plates, and radio intercept fragments made available to authorized digital investigators. Progression through evidence stages required to unlock restricted sections.
