DOCUMENT ID: DYA-DIARY-01 | RECOVERY: TENT SITE

Dyatlov Personal Diary — Early Entries

23 January 1959

Preparations nearly complete. The group is strong. Yuri, Zina, Rustem, all experienced. Yet I find myself writing the same line twice: something about this journey feels different. I cannot explain the unease. Perhaps the isolation. Perhaps the stories the old hunters tell about Kholat Syakhl.

We leave the day after tomorrow. I will not share these doubts with the others.

Final Diary Fragment — Recovered

1 February 1959 — evening

Wind stronger than expected. We chose the slope rather than drop to the timberline. Mistake? The others are settling. I keep thinking of the word I wrote weeks ago: unease. It has not left.

There is a sound tonight that is not the wind. Or perhaps it is only the wind. I will finish this entry in the morning.
